## Brand & Style

This design system drives a peer-to-peer educational network and global student hub. The visual narrative balances intellectual rigor with digital-native vitality, catering to ambitious Gen-Z and collegiate builders, self-directed researchers, and international study syndicates. 

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Kinetic Minimalism** with vibrant, high-contrast digital accents. Crisp white canvases and deep slate structures anchor an energetic palette of electric violet, radiant coral, sun-drenched orange, and focused emerald. Surfaces feel tactically lightweight, optimistic, and purposeful—avoiding sterile academic dryness as well as juvenile gamification tropes. High typographic clarity, hyper-curated interest tags, verified trust indicators, and layered social micro-interactions create an atmosphere of momentum, peer support, and intellectual discovery.

## Colors

The color system functions on crisp polarity: hyper-pigmented energetic accents balanced over structured dark slate tones and radiant, airy backdrops.

- **Primary (`#6C5CE7` - Electric Violet):** Drives primary actions, active navigation states, platform-level milestones, and core interactive chrome.
- **Secondary (`#FF7675` - Hyper Coral):** Reserved for peer engagement, study room pings, reaction streaks, spotlight calls, and high-urgency badges.
- **Tertiary (`#FD9644` - Radiant Orange):** Denotes active challenges, daily achievements, collaborative events, and creative discipline tracks.
- **Eco & Verification Green (`#00B894` - Emerald Vitality):** Expresses expert/mentor verification tags, sustainability tracks, success states, and live peer activity status.
- **Neutral Dark (`#0F172A` - Midnight Slate):** Applied for high-contrast typography, critical headers, structural container borders, and nocturnal focus modes.
- **Surface Foundations:** Crisp canvas layers start at pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with secondary backdrops resting on soft lavender-tinted porcelain (`#F8F9FD`) and border outlines rendered in structural cool zinc (`#E2E8F0`).

Functional contrast ratios consistently meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AA for interface controls and AAA for high-density academic body text.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples **Plus Jakarta Sans** with **Space Grotesk**. 

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** supplies conversational warmth, contemporary geometric balance, and maximum readability across deep curriculum briefs, peer discussions, and main marketing headings.
- **Space Grotesk** serves as a high-precision, technical counterpart for meta-information, tag badges, statistics, verification labels, dates, and live session counts. Its slightly quirky mechanical ink-traps signal technical acumen and futuristic focus.

Headings maintain tight negative tracking to keep vibrant display sizes grounded, while meta labels use positive letter spacing and uppercased/tabular layouts to retain legibility in compact pill badges.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an adaptable 12-column responsive fluid grid structured around an 8pt spatial baseline:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximizes density for multi-pane study sessions, peer feeds, and synchronized note modules.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Collapses secondary sidebars into responsive swipeable overlays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Transforms feeds into unified single-column vertical flows with persistent quick-action navigation rails.

Spacing tokens dictate clear structural separation between social interaction tiers and academic content cards, maintaining rapid scanning speeds without cognitive fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages crisp surfaces, selective tonal tinting, and colored ambient diffusion over aggressive shadow stacking:

- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas):** Underlying canvas rendered in `#F8F9FD`.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by hairline borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Hover):** Lifted cards acquire an energetic ambient glow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(108, 92, 231, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals, Study Drawers, Popovers):** Elevated overlays sit atop backdrop blur filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with crisp borders and deep ambient suspension: `0 20px 35px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.
- **Accent Glows:** Status elements (live audio rooms, peer study sessions) use targeted accent halos using translucent fills corresponding to their state (e.g., `#00B894` at 15% opacity).

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy combines structural stability with rounded modern tactility:

- **Standard Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Academic posts, project files, and interactive feed modules utilize rounded corners that feel friendly and approachable yet clean.
- **Input Fields & Dialogue Frames (8px):** Retain structured geometric definition for effortless eye tracking and data entry.
- **Pills, Badges & Chips (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Interest tags, verification marks, active filters, and interactive reaction triggers are rendered as full-geometry pills, contrasting with the rectangular structure of surrounding content modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#6C5CE7` with pure white text, pill-shaped or rounded (`10px`), with subtle scale transition on press. Focus ring is an offset `2px` ring of `#6C5CE7`.
- **Accent Action:** Vibrant `#FF7675` background with white text for study group invites, live room joins, and celebratory reactions.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#E2E8F0`, slate `#0F172A` text, shifting to soft lilac tint (`#6C5CE7` at 6% fill) on hover.

### Badges & Verification Indicators
- **Verified Student:** Pill badge featuring a micro graduation cap icon; emerald background `#00B894` at 12% opacity with solid `#008f72` label text in `Space Grotesk`.
- **Subject Matter Expert / Mentor:** Electric violet badge (`#6C5CE7` at 12% opacity) paired with a solid `#6C5CE7` verified spark icon and bold label.
- **Sustainability / Eco Topic Badge:** Pill badge in vibrant `#00B894` with a crisp leaf icon, used across ecological course tracks and research topics.

### Interest Tags & Chips
- Fully rounded pills (`padding: 6px 14px`) utilizing pale pastel tint backdrops matched to content categories (e.g., `#FD9644` at 10% for Design, `#6C5CE7` at 10% for Computer Science).
- Active or selectable tags invert to full-bleed fills with white typography.

### Social & Community Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` backgrounds, `1px solid #E2E8F0` outlines, and 16px corner radii.
- Headers integrate author avatar, name in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold, alongside inline verification chips and time metadata.
- Bottom interaction bar houses reaction counters, study session join prompts, and bookmark toggles with energetic color fills upon engagement.

### Input Fields
- `1.5px` border in `#E2E8F0`, `#FFFFFF` interior, `Plus Jakarta Sans` body typography.
- Shifts cleanly on focus to a `#6C5CE7` border with a subtle 3px glow (`rgba(108, 92, 231, 0.15)`).

### Navigation Chrome
- High-contrast, crystal-clear top navigation featuring frosted translucent acrylic styling (`#FFFFFF` at 85% opacity, `blur(12px)`).
- Active nav links display an electric violet underline indicator paired with semi-bold typography.
